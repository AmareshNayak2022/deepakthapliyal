About Deepak

A Priest Who Explains, Not Just Performs

Hindu priest Deepak Thapliyal has spent over fifteen years conducting pujas, weddings and life-cycle ceremonies for families across the United Kingdom. Trained in Vedic scripture from an early age, he carries fluency not only in the mantras themselves but in what they mean — translating each ritual into language a British-born generation can carry forward.

Every ceremony he leads, whether an intimate griha pravesh in a new flat or a three-day wedding celebration, is conducted with the same unhurried care: the right materials, the correct order of rites, and time set aside afterward for questions.

  • Fluent in Sanskrit, Hindi and English
  • Trained across North Indian and Gujarati wedding traditions
  • Available for ceremonies across the UK and destination bookings abroad
  • Known for a calm, patient, family-friendly approach
